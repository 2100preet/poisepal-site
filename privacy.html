Privacy Policy

Draft: This page is an outline, not a final privacy policy. The final text will be published before launch.

Last updated: to be published before launch

Outline to complete

  • Who we are: legal entity, address and contact email (to be added).
  • What PoisePal processes: meeting audio while a session is active (after you press Start), transcripts, meeting briefs, and summaries. Briefs, transcripts, summaries and call history are stored on your device.
  • Your own AI keys: audio and text are sent directly from your device to the providers you configure (for example Deepgram, OpenAI or Google Gemini) under their terms.
  • Hosted AI (coming with launch): audio streams from your device directly to Deepgram using a short-lived token. Suggestion and summary requests pass through PoisePal’s server (Cloudflare) to OpenAI or Google Gemini and are not stored or logged by PoisePal. PoisePal’s server stores your email, plan and monthly usage counts (minutes and requests). Sign-in codes are sent by email. Subprocessors, retention, regions and training terms to be listed here.
  • Website: this site has no sign-up form and collects no personal information beyond standard hosting logs.
  • Cookies and analytics: the site currently sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts.
  • Your rights: access, deletion, and GDPR/CCPA rights as applicable (details to be added).
  • Call participants: users are responsible for obtaining consent from call participants where the law requires it.
